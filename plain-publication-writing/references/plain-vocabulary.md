# Plain Vocabulary

Replace inflated or complex words with basic ones. Never replace field-specific technical terms, named methods, or instruments.

## Phrases to Avoid

- "delve into" -> study, examine
- "plays a crucial/pivotal/vital role in" -> is important for, helps
- "it is worth noting that", "it is important to note that", "interestingly" -> delete and state the fact directly
- "sheds light on" -> shows, explains
- "a growing body of literature" -> recent studies
- "in the realm of" -> in
- "stands as a testament to" -> shows
- "in today's world", "in the modern era" -> delete
- "groundbreaking", "novel", "cutting-edge", "comprehensive", "robust" -> keep only when accurate and needed; otherwise delete, or use "new", "strong"

## Never Change

- Field-specific technical terms, named methods, instruments, and models
- Numbers, units, p-values, confidence intervals, and statistics
- Citation keys and reference entries
- Equations and their symbols
- Species names, gene names, and other required italics
